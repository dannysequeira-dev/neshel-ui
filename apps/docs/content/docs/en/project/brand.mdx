**Neshel-UI** — *Rooted in faith. Built with hope.*

The identity is a navy-blue rose with subtle golden reflections and a soft light at its centre,
representing faith, hope and care. The default theme translates it into midnight and navy blues,
ivory surfaces and a restrained gold accent.

| Token (light)          | Value     | Role                              |
| ---------------------- | --------- | --------------------------------- |
| `--neshel-background`  | `#fbf9f6` | Ivory canvas                      |
| `--neshel-foreground`  | `#0d1b33` | Midnight text                     |
| `--neshel-primary`     | `#193767` | Navy primary actions              |
| `--neshel-accent`      | `#c49a59` | Restrained gold                   |
| `--neshel-focus`       | `#8f6d3f` | Gold-bronze focus ring            |

## Your application's identity

The default appearance belongs to Neshel-UI, but your application owns its own: change every colour,
font and radius through [`neshel.config.ts`](/en/docs/guides/theming/). Nothing in the components
depends on navy or gold.

## Logo and brand assets

The canonical logo is a raster PNG (1254 × 1254) with an ivory background; the mark variants are crops
and resizes of it. They are not vector artwork and must not be redrawn, recoloured or altered.

The MIT licence covers the software. It does **not** grant rights to use the Neshel-UI name or logo
in ways that suggest endorsement or affiliation. A brand asset usage policy is being prepared for the
first release; until it is published, please ask the maintainers before using the logo outside of
references to the project.
