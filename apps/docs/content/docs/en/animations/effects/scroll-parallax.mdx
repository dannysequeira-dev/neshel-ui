These effects map the native scroll position to a property. Geometry is cached and re-read only when it changes, pinning is CSS `position: sticky`, and nothing hijacks scrolling. Effects inside a `<ScrollScene>` share its timeline; others create their own, for the page or for a `source` container. An ancestor with `overflow: hidden`, `auto` or `scroll` becomes the sticky container: use `overflow: clip` instead.

## SP01 · Depth parallax [#sp01]

`<ScrollLayer>` moves a layer of a scene by `distance` multiplied by `depth`, on either `axis`. Keep text in layers with a depth close to 1 so that it stays readable.

<Effect id="SP01" />

## SP02 · Scrubbed transform [#sp02]

`scrubTransform` interpolates between two poses (`translateX`, `translateY`, `rotateZ`, `scale`, `opacity`) as the element crosses the viewport, with an `easing`. Scrolling back reverses it exactly.

<Effect id="SP02" />

## SP03 · Scroll progress bar [#sp03]

`<ScrollProgressBar>` is a decorative, `aria-hidden` bar for the page or a `source`; `position` places it at the top, the bottom or in the flow. `scrollProgress` writes the same value as `--neshel-scroll-progress`.

<Effect id="SP03" />

## SP04 · Horizontal section [#sp04]

`<HorizontalScroll>` turns vertical scrolling into horizontal travel of a pinned track. `layout` can be `"pinned"`, `"stacked"` or `"scroll"` (a native scroller, used in the example). Focus keeps items in view; under reduced motion it is stacked. Put something focusable in the items: the native scroller is not a tab stop by itself.

<Effect id="SP04" />

## SP05 · Pinned scene [#sp05]

`<ScrollScene>` pins a stage for `length` viewport heights and exposes `--neshel-scene-progress` to everything inside. `useScrollScene()` gives the timeline to other effects, such as scene chapters.

<Effect id="SP05" />

## SP06 · Image parallax [#sp06]

`<ParallaxImage>` moves the first child inside a clipping frame by ±`amount` pixels and scales it just enough to cover the frame. The native image keeps its attributes.

<Effect id="SP06" />

## SP07 · Zoom on scroll [#sp07]

`zoomOnScroll` scales the element between `from` (1) and `to` (1.2) over the chosen range. It composes with other transforms on the same element.

<Effect id="SP07" />

## SP08 · Scroll colour shift [#sp08]

`scrollColorShift` mixes 2–8 `stops` into `--neshel-scroll-color`, or sets `data-neshel-theme` on a target with `themes`. Check the contrast of your text at every stop; under reduced motion the change is stepped.

<Effect id="SP08" />

## SP09 · Sticky stack [#sp09]

`<StickyStack>` makes its children sticky cards that pile up; `top` and `offset` are CSS lengths and `scale` shrinks covered cards. Cards taller than the viewport cannot be read completely.

<Effect id="SP09" />

## SP10 · Smooth scrub [#sp10]

The `smooth` option of `<ScrollScene>`, `useScrollProgress` and `createScrollProgress` passes progress through a spring. The page scroll itself is never smoothed; only what follows it is.

<Effect id="SP10" />

## SP11 · Velocity skew [#sp11]

`velocitySkew` leans the element with the scroll velocity, limited by `max` degrees, and springs back to zero. Keep it small: it is decorative and turns off under reduced motion.

<Effect id="SP11" />
