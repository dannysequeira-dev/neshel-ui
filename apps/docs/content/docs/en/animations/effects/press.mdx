Press effects respond to real activation, pointer or keyboard, and never change what the control does. `<Press>` accepts one preset or a list. Triggered effects (burst, shake, nudge) play when their `trigger` value changes after mounting; the initial value never plays. `ToggleMorph` and `CheckboxDraw` are decorative visuals for inside your own labelled control. Disabled elements never react.

## P01 · Press scale [#p01]

`pressScale` shrinks the control while it is held: `scale` (0.96, between 0.5 and 1) and `spring`. Under reduced motion half of the amplitude remains, without the spring, because it confirms the press.

<Effect id="P01" />

## P02 · Ripple [#p02]

`ripple` expands a circle from the point of contact, or from the centre with the keyboard: `color`, `opacity` (0.22) and `duration` (550 ms). It draws on a child layer owned by the package.

<Effect id="P02" />

## P03 · Tap bounce [#p03]

`tapBounce` answers a completed tap with a spring bounce: `strength` (0.3) and `spring`. It plays after the activation, so it never delays the action.

<Effect id="P03" />

## P04 · Toggle morph [#p04]

`<ToggleMorph>` draws the track and thumb of a switch and stretches the thumb as `checked` changes (`stretch` 0.3). It has no semantics: put it inside a `role="switch"` button with a name. For a complete control use the Components Switch.

<Effect id="P04" />

## P05 · Checkbox draw [#p05]

`<CheckboxDraw>` draws the mark of a checkbox with `checked` and `indeterminate` (`duration` 240 ms). The native input keeps the semantics and the keyboard; hide it visually, not from assistive technology.

<Effect id="P05" />

## P06 · Burst [#p06]

`burst` throws particles from the element when `trigger` changes: `count` (10), `distance` (34 px), `size` and `colors`. Celebrate rarely; announce the result with text.

<Effect id="P06" />

## P07 · Shake [#p07]

`shake` moves the element sideways when `trigger` changes: `distance` (8 px), `cycles` (4) and `duration` (420 ms). The error must also be text with `role="alert"` or `aria-describedby`; the shake is never the only signal.

<Effect id="P07" />

## P08 · Nudge [#p08]

`nudge` rotates the element briefly to attract attention: `angle` (6°), `cycles` (3) and at most three `repeat`s separated by `interval`. It plays on `trigger` changes, never in an endless loop.

<Effect id="P08" />

## P09 · Number tick [#p09]

`<NumberTick>` counts from the previous `value` to the new one. `locale`, `formatOptions` or your own `format` produce the text; the accessible value does not change on every frame, and `live` opts into an announcement.

<Effect id="P09" />

## P10 · Content swap [#p10]

`<ContentSwap>` swaps its children for `confirm` while `active` is true — "Copy link" and "Copied" — in one stacked cell, so the width does not jump. `live` announces the confirmation.

<Effect id="P10" />

## P11 · Long press [#p11]

`<LongPress>` calls `onLongPress` once after holding for `duration` (600 ms), with a progress fill; releasing early cancels it. Holding Space or Enter works too, or a single press with `keyboard: "press"`. Offer another path for destructive actions.

<Effect id="P11" />
